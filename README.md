I'm Stephen Willcock, Vice President and Chief Architect at [Certinia](https://certinia.com) (formerly FinancialForce). I've been building enterprise applications on the Salesforce platform since 2007, and I was part of the founding development team at FinancialForce.com. Before becoming Chief Architect I led Product Innovation for six years: researching and assessing new technologies, and prototyping new products and features. My team created the open source [Apex Log Analyzer](https://github.com/certinia/debug-log-analyzer), a debug log analyzer for Salesforce developers in VS Code.

> "He simplifies complex situations in a way no one else I've ever met can do." - Deb Ashton, founder of Certinia

Outside work I design and build 8-bit computers, and write about them as [PainfulDiodes](https://painfuldiodes.com).
